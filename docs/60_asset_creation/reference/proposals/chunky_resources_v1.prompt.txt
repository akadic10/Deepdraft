Technical visual language: visibly axis-aligned cubic voxel construction; the original resources occupy about 12 micro-voxels per axis within a 16-cell canvas, one cell = 1/16 world block. Merge adjacent same-colored cubes visually into broad readable planes. Chunkiness comes from exaggerated main masses, controlled asymmetry, thick chips and simplified readable material marks. Matte surfaces, consistent soft neutral studio light, clear contact shadows; identical lighting in both rows so shape improvement is visible. Beautiful tangible voxel maquettes, not photorealistic rocks, not watercolor, no smoothed geometry or bevel shader, no faces, no mascot eyes, no characters, no terrain textures.
