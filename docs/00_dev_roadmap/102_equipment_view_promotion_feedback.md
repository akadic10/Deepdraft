# 102 — Equipment view and promotion feedback

Implemented 2026-10-09. The small tool summary under the dwarf's profession was
not sufficient to explain what was equipped or how progression would work.

## Player flow

- Select a dwarf in **Colony → Dwarves**, or inspect them in the world, then open
  **Equipment**. Both surfaces use the same read-only view.
- Carpenter shows the real **Crude Carpentry Kit**, its thumbnail, crude tier,
  wooden mallet/stone adze materials and actual benefit: enables Carpenter,
  with no work-speed bonus. It stays equipped until changing professions.
- **Iron saw** is shown as a planned Blacksmith upgrade, explicitly **Not
  craftable yet**. The planned flow is to craft a better tool, assign it in the
  Equipment view, then have the dwarf collect it and return the old tool.
- Miner shows the included default pickaxe and planned iron pickaxe. Its future
  chance to mine 2–3 marked voxels is separate from the current experience bonus.
- Worker explains Rudimentary starter-tool crafting and Carpenter promotion.
- The profession appointment card points to Equipment for the next tool.

Upgrade items, recipes, assignment controls, equipment bonuses, automatic
upgrading, durability and specialist workshops are not implemented here.

## Ownership and presentation

`DwarfInspection.equipment_details()` reads the actual equipped item key from
`DwarfEquipment`, item details from ItemDropManager's registry, and profession
preview copy from DwarfAssets. No UI writes equipment or inventory state.
`DwarfEquipmentPanel` presents those fields; `DwarfInspectorPanel` embeds it in
both inspector hosts. The narrow Colony column uses two rows of navigation.
On short screens it hides the duplicate tool summary to retain usable scrolling.

The original 1.25-second promotion chime is generated by
`tools/generate_promotion_audio.py` into `assets/audio/ui/promotion.wav` using
three warm synthesized mallet tones. It contains no external samples.
`WorkFeedback` owns the JSON-configured bank/profile and one nonspatial player
on the existing Work bus, respecting its volume and mute controls. A 0.2-second
debounce bounds rapid repeats; scene cleanup stops and frees playback.

`DwarfAgent._complete_profession_change()` triggers the cue only when changing
to a specialist. Queuing or cancelling collection, failed requests, repeated
assignment, returning to Worker and restoring a save do not trigger it.
The cue is audible outside the camera/slice and for immediate promotion while
the world clock is paused. It has no saved state and consumes no simulation RNG.

## Verification

- `EquipmentFeedbackTest`: actual kit ownership, request/cancel/completion,
  pause/resume, no replay on restore or repeated requests, bounded playback and
  cleanup. Real Equipment clicks in both hosts at 960×540, 1280×720 and 2560×1440;
  browsing preserves stock/profession and the narrow body remains usable.
- Native screenshots in `tmp/equipment_ui_review/` reviewed for both hosts,
  including scrolling to future upgrades.
- `PromotionAudioPlaybackTest`: records game output on Master (no microphone).
  Normal and paused peak 0.185; quieter Work volume peak 0.026; mute and scene
  cleanup produce zero PCM. There is ample clipping headroom and no camera gate.
- ToolPromotion, DwarfInspector, DwarfRoster, DwarfProfessionPanel, WorkFeedback
  and RabbitAudio regression tests pass. Fixtures use isolated user-data folders.

No save-format changes or old-save compatibility paths are introduced.
