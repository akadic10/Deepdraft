# 168 — Environmental ambience

Implemented 2026-10-13: a first woodland soundscape fills the quiet between
local work, wildlife, water and fire sounds.

Follow-up [170 — Environment sound details](170_environment_sound_details.md)
completes the remaining contextual sounds and adds rain/storm weather. The
first-pass scope and measurements below remain the historical record.

## Listening experience

- Broad stereo wind remains audible at overview zoom. Nearby live, visible
  trees add foliage rustle; removing trees removes their contribution.
- Six intermittent bird phrases follow daylight, with shorter gaps around
  the same seasonal sunrise anchors as the sky. Dusk introduces insects.
- Warm nights use a restrained stereo cricket bed and three occasional owl
  phrases. Spring/autumn reduce insects; winter and snow remove them.
- Overcast strengthens wind and reduces wildlife calls. Fog reduces wind,
  rustle and calls; snow uses a quieter palette. These are the four existing
  weather types; rain and thunder are not fabricated under overcast.
- The viewed column samples physical sky access through UndergroundLighting.
  Enclosed spaces replace outside sounds with a very quiet sheltered-air bed.
  Removing a roof visually with Slice does not introduce outdoor sound.
- Four-second gain transitions avoid hard boundaries. Pause fades the beds
  to 25% and stops discrete calls. Playback and phrase timers use real time;
  simulation speed never changes pitch or compresses the soundscape.

The first pass does not add simulated songbird/owl entities, wolf howls,
shoreline laps, frogs, cave drips, branch creaks, music or new weather types.
Those remain separate contextual refinements. Existing actual animal sounds,
moving water and installed fire playback retain their current behavior.

## Ownership and controls

`AmbientAudio` is an autoload after SaveManager and the existing clock, weather
and WorkFeedback owners. It owns `data/audio/environment.json`, the Ambience
bus, four stereo loop players and a two-voice spatial call pool. Only an active
world camera in `work_audio_view` opts into playback. Cosmetic random choices
never consume simulation/weather RNG or serialize into a settlement.

The controller clears players and node references synchronously on scene exit,
loading or loss of its listening view. A restored scene builds one fresh pool.
Asset viewers and unrelated test scenes do not acquire ambience.

Menu → Audio offers separate Ambience and Effects sliders, mute buttons and
Restore defaults. Effects controls the existing Work bus, including water,
fire, work, actual wildlife and notifications. The original Effects default
of -7 dB is retained. Preferences debounce to `user://audio.cfg`, owned only
by AmbientAudio, and apply across settlements without changing save files.

All 13 new WAVs are original seeded synthesis, generated by
`tools/generate_environment_audio.py`; no external recordings were used.
Four seamless stereo loops have different 23–37 second lengths. Six bird
phrases and three owl phrases have tapered endpoints, private randomized
placement/intervals and no immediate repetition. Existing sounds are untouched.
