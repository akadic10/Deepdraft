# 119 — Dwarf walking and ground rest

Implemented 2026-10-11 following playtest feedback about slow walking and standing sleep.

This milestone covers the ground-rest fallback. Bed use, ownership and sleep
comfort subsequently shipped in [120](120_log_beds_and_bedrooms.md),
[121](121_personal_bedrooms.md) and [122](122_bedroom_comfort_and_mining_progression.md).

- Normal walking: 2.2 → 3.4 blocks/second; stride: 0.7 → 1.05 blocks. Longer,
  eased foot arcs, planted stance and torso/head/hand counter-motion. Simulation
  speed now applies consistently to normal walks and bridge/ladder exits.
- Sleep interrupts and releases work/cargo using the existing protocol, then
  searches nearby for a wall or tree. Only proven local flat routes are used.
  Whole-pose clearance and exclusive transient footprints prevent clipping into
  obstacles or selecting another sleeper's space. Bridges, ladder approaches,
  stockpiles and furniture plans are excluded; marked trees are avoided.
- Reclining and seated poses have settle/wake transitions, closed voxel eyelids,
  breathing and slow head motion. Open ground is a fallback, and cramped spaces
  use compact sitting. This original ground-rest change adds no bed or comfort mechanic.
- Three original snore recordings are generated by `tools/generate_snore_audio.py`.
  The shared Work bus and voice pool control volume/mute and distance/zoom.
  Hidden/sliced, awake and paused dwarves are silent; at most two snores overlap.
- Existing sleep fields remain authoritative. Travel does not consume the six
  hours of rest. Restored sleepers rebuild their pose without resetting need;
  terrain support loss and shelter removal cause safe reselection. Navigation
  root transforms remain supported; only visual parts recline.

Ownership: TaskManager loads `movement` and `rest` tuning from task_config.json;
DwarfAgent retains scheduling/needs; DwarfRestBehavior owns rest search and pose;
WorkFeedback loads audio definitions. No new autoload or save schema is needed.

Verification: DwarfRestTest exercises real wall/tree selection, both poses,
four orientations, claim separation, mirrored limbs/floor clearance, save restore,
pause/speed, wake/reassignment, shelter/floor removal, fallback and native audio.
Native captures and PCM measurements are under `tmp/dwarf_rest_20261011`.
HaulingAnimationTest, DwarfIdleBehaviorTest, DwarfSupportTest,
BridgeCornerWalkingTest, LadderTest, DwarfInspectorTest, DwarfRosterTest and
SaveManagerRoundTripTest cover integration and existing lifecycle behavior.
