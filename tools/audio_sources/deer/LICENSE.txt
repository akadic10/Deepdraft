SELECTION: Deer Meewing 01.wav — msantoro11
https://freesound.org/people/msantoro11/sounds/351113/
The author describes calls made by a group of deer at a nature park.
Three separate calls provide the deer selection variations. The recording
does not identify the deer species, age or sex. No artificial voice is added.

GRAZING FOLEY: Horse Eating — gibarroule
https://freesound.org/people/gibarroule/sounds/365138/
The author describes a horse eating and chewing grass outdoors at Kuhhorst,
Germany, recorded with a Sennheiser shotgun microphone and Zoom H4N.
Short grass-chewing excerpts represent deer grazing. This is horse foley,
not a recording of a deer eating.
