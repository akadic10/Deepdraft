NIGHT LOOP: Crickets_Clean.wav — johnnydekk
https://freesound.org/people/johnnydekk/sounds/626180/
The author isolated the cricket and removed background noise from an earlier
field recording. The earlier recording identifies a Zoom H4N recorder:
https://freesound.org/people/johnnydekk/sounds/436510/
Stereo is preserved. A 1.5-second tail-to-head crossfade makes a 37-second loop.

DAY LOOP: Cicadas — floft
https://freesound.org/people/floft/sounds/527812/
Cicadas recorded in the University of Idaho Experimental Forest on Zoom H2N
M/S microphones with a Rode windscreen, decoded to stereo in Ardour by the
author. Stereo is preserved. A 1.5-second crossfade makes a 31-second loop.

CHIRPS: Single Cricket Chirping, Close Perspective 02 — HECKFRICKER
https://freesound.org/people/HECKFRICKER/sounds/753272/
A cricket on a rose bush, recorded with a Tascam DR-05X. The author isolated
background noise and normalized the recording in iZotope RX 9. Three separate
pairs of chirps are mixed to mono, retaining their original spacing and pitch.

Masters were decoded from Freesound's public HQ MP3 previews, not downloaded
from the original lossless upload endpoints. source.json records download
hashes, master conversion, crop positions, filters, crossfades, fades and levels.
Preparation resamples with anti-alias filtering to 22050 Hz. Loops overlap only
at the crossfade; no synthetic oscillators, time stretching, pitch shifting,
added reverb, or artificial stereo widening is used.
