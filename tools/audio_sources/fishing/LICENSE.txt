Each local master is a decoded copy of Freesound's public HQ MP3 preview, not the original
lossless upload (the originals need a Freesound login). source.json records each download
URL, the hashes, the conversion and the edit regions; tools/generate_fishing_audio.py rebuilds
the game's cues from these masters without a download.

Substitutes, named as such: the float landing is pebbles falling into a pond, not a float; the
landing is a fish splashing as it is released back into the water, not one being pulled out; the
cast is a bamboo rod swung through the air, without a line. No synthetic layers, time stretching
or pitch changes are baked into any cue.
