SELECTION AND GRAZING: A rabbit is eating a cucumber — davi-sh
https://freesound.org/people/davi-sh/sounds/482732/
The author identifies this as a European rabbit eating cucumber, recorded
with a Zoom H5 and normalized to -3 dB. All six variations use different,
non-overlapping excerpts of this actual rabbit recording.

Selection uses short nibble/mouth sounds as quiet interaction foley. Grazing
uses longer chewing phrases. These are cucumber-eating sounds used to
represent grazing, not a field recording of a wild rabbit eating grass.
No animal vocalization or synthetic squeak is added.

The master was decoded from Freesound's public HQ MP3 preview, not downloaded
from the original-upload WAV endpoint. source.json records download hashes,
master conversion, crop positions, filters, fades and levels. Preparation does
not shift pitch, stretch time, synthesize voices or add reverb.
