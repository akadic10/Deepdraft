Preview: https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/117/117504_181941-hq.mp3
Local master: window_wind.wav
Variable cold wind with a slight whistle, recorded beside a partially open window in Toronto; separate excerpts supply winter wind and filtered sheltered air.

Masters were decoded from public HQ MP3 previews, not the original lossless uploads. They retain supplied stereo channels and were resampled to 22050 Hz PCM16 with anti-alias filtering and joint gain. The window-wind master retains the first 90 seconds. Source and master hashes, source regions, gains and the five output edit profiles are in source.json.

General wind, tree foliage and autumn leaves each use a different field recording. Winter wind and sheltered air use separate excerpts from the same real window-wind recording. Sheltered air is a filtered adaptation, not a recording of a cave.
