A crackling log fire with moving flames, recorded on Zoom H1. A quieter excerpt is adapted as torch foley; this is recorded wood fire, not a claim of an actual torch recording.

Masters were decoded from public HQ MP3 previews, not original lossless uploads. Stereo sources were averaged to mono and resampled to 22050 Hz with anti-alias filtering, retaining natural speed and pitch, then stored as PCM16. Both masters retain their complete source recording. Crops, hashes, gains and reproducible edit profiles are in source.json.

Campfire also supplies the hearth, per existing game definitions. Its output uses tanh soft peak control at four times source RMS to tame close-mic wood pops, before loop crossfading and gain. Torch uses a quieter passage of a separate log-fire recording as foley for a small stationary flame; it is not a recording of a torch. The original source by TheWoodlandNomad was formerly attributed to FractalStudios. Source descriptions distinguish uploader processing from our filters and loop crossfades.
