HOWLS: wolves.wav — Paresh
https://freesound.org/people/Paresh/sounds/158780/
The author identifies these as wolves recorded at Wolf Haven International,
a wolf sanctuary in Washington State. This is a pack recording; excerpts can
contain more than one wolf. No synthetic howl or human imitation is added.

SELECTION FOLEY: Dog sniffing.wav — jedg
https://freesound.org/people/jedg/sounds/505821/
The author identifies the animal as a Dalmatian. Short recorded sniff/snort
excerpts represent a nearby wolf's quiet selection cue. This is dog foley,
not a recording of a wolf.

EATING FOLEY: Dog eats — 16FVolejnikovaA
https://freesound.org/people/16FVolejnikovaA/sounds/498396/
The author identifies this as a dog eating its meal. Short canine chewing
excerpts represent wolf feeding. This is dog foley, not a recording of a wolf.
