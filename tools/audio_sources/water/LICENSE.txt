Local master: water_splashes.wav
Recorded splashes in a lake, captured with Zoom H4N Pro and processed by the uploader with accusonus Noise Remover. Excerpts used as duck splash foley; the source does not claim recorded ducks.

Masters are decoded from public HQ MP3 previews, not original lossless uploads. Shoreline remains stereo; cave drips retain the left microphone to avoid cancellation, and moving-water/duck foley sources are averaged to mono for spatial playback. Sources retain their natural speed and pitch. Duck splashes stay at 48000 Hz; other sources are converted to 22050 Hz with anti-alias filtering. All masters use PCM16.

The three cave cues are short phrases from an actual cave field recording, with natural reverberation and background drips. Duck cues are excerpts of recorded splashes in lake water, used as foley; no claim that the source recorded ducks. River output uses soft peak control to keep close-mic transients from making the whole bed too quiet. Crops, channel choices, conversion, gains, hashes and reproducible output edit profiles are documented in source.json.
