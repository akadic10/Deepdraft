DAYTIME BIRDS: Blackbird (isolated) — richwise
https://freesound.org/people/richwise/sounds/811988/
The author describes a close blackbird singing in the evening, recorded with
the XY microphones of a Zoom H5 Studio. Very distant traffic is noted in the
source description. Six separate song phrases provide the daytime bird bank;
they are variations of one bird species, not six different species.

OWLS: Perfect Spooky Owls — DreamSavvyr
https://freesound.org/people/DreamSavvyr/sounds/787733/
The author describes real outdoor owl hoots recorded with a Tascam DR-05,
with pauses between calls shortened in the uploaded recording. The owl
species is not identified by the author. Three separate hooting sequences
are used; no human imitation or synthetic owl voice is added.

Masters were decoded from Freesound's public HQ MP3 previews, not downloaded
from the original-upload WAV endpoints. source.json records download hashes,
master conversion, crop positions, filters, fades and levels. Preparation
mixes to mono and resamples with anti-alias filtering to 22050 Hz while
preserving original speed and pitch. No time stretching or reverb is added.
