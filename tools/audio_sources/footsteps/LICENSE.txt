Six grass-footstep foley events: the uploader layered a recorded thud with moving grass, using a Zoom H4n and Audacity.

Public HQ MP3 previews were decoded, not the original lossless uploads. Complete previews are retained as mono 48 kHz PCM16 masters. source.json records download/master hashes and exact edit regions. Stone and wood are direct boot recordings. Grass is recorded foley layered by the uploader. Gravel is filtered and used as earth-path foley, not a claim of a separate compacted-earth recording. Our preparation trims individual steps, filters, applies short edge fades and adjusts gain; it adds no synthesis or reverberation and does not change speed or pitch.
