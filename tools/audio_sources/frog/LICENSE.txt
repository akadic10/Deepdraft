FROGS: Small Frog Croaks — clif_creates
https://freesound.org/people/clif_creates/sounds/802737/
The author describes one small frog isolated from a group with a shotgun
microphone in Ojai, California. The uploaded recording already has iZotope RX
noise reduction. The species is not identified. Three separate, complete
croaks provide the variations; no synthetic or human-imitation voice is used.

The master was decoded from Freesound's public HQ MP3 preview, not downloaded
from the original-upload WAV endpoint. source.json records download hashes,
master conversion, crop positions, filters, fades and levels. Preparation
resamples with anti-alias filtering to mono 22050 Hz while preserving original
speed and pitch. No time stretching, rearrangement or reverb is added.
