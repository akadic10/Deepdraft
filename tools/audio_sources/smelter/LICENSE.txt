Each local master is a decoded copy of Freesound's public HQ MP3 preview, not the original
lossless upload (the originals need a Freesound login). source.json records each download
URL, the hashes, the conversion and the edit regions; tools/generate_smelter_audio.py rebuilds
the game's cues and its loop from these masters without a download.

Substitutes, named as such: the log going into the fire hole is logs thrown onto other logs; two of
the three breaths are blown through a cardboard tube and the third through a plastic pipe, none of
them at a fire; the copper bar is an aluminium ingot dropped on concrete; the smelter's fire is the
fire of a wood-fired pottery kiln. The lumps are rocks thrown onto rocks, as in the game. No
synthetic layers, time stretching or pitch changes are baked into any cue.
