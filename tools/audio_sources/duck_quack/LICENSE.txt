The source page identifies this as a real duck recording made in Kuhhorst,
Germany, and releases it under CC0. Retrieved 2026-10-28.

duck_quacks.wav is a decoded, uniformly level-adjusted copy of Freesound's
public HQ MP3 preview. It is not the original 96 kHz upload. source.json
records the download URL, hashes, conversion and production edit regions.

The three game quacks are separate excerpts, lightly filtered, faded and
level-matched by tools/generate_duck_audio.py. No synthetic voice layers,
time stretching or pitch changes are baked into them.
