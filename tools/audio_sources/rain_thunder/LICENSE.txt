Masters were decoded from public HQ MP3 previews, not original lossless uploads, and resampled to 22050 Hz PCM16 with anti-alias filtering. Rain retains stereo; thunder retains one of the author's microphone channels because averaging the two would cancel much of the rumble. Roof-rain master retains the first 80 seconds. Source/master hashes, channel choices, source regions, gains and output edit profiles are in source.json.

Rain and roof rain are separate recordings. The three thunder variants are three separate real thunder events, not pitch-shifted copies. They were recorded after rain had passed; the author reduced wind and bird noise. Natural pitch and timing are retained; loops are crossfaded and thunder boundaries faded.

These three are separate close thunder events with a sharp crack, used for lightning strikes near the colony. Like the others they were decoded from public HQ MP3 previews, not the original lossless uploads (which need a Freesound login), and resampled to 22050 Hz PCM16; one microphone channel is retained. Natural pitch and timing are kept; only trimming, edge fades, filtering and gain are applied.
